AI goes to the data. The data stays put.

Sovereignty does not depend on a contract with a vendor: it is built into the architecture.

The usual way

Data is copied to the vendor's cloud so that a model can process it.

With Software in a Box

The model runs where the data lives. Only the result you authorise leaves.

Machine-readable rules

Every use of the data is governed by an ODRL contract, and every access is logged and auditable.

European standards

Gaia-X, Dataspace Protocol, HealthDCAT-AP and EHDS: any member can connect without depending on a single vendor.

Compliance by design

GDPR, the AI Act, EHDS, NIS2 and the Data Act are addressed in the architecture, not afterwards.

Hardware in your own room

Servers are installed on your premises or in your data centre, with no dependence on hyperscalers.

Three real data spaces behind the product

The Software in a Box team has taken part in the technology development of three data spaces co-funded by Spain's Secretariat of State for Digitalisation and Artificial Intelligence (SEDIA).

  • Global Data CareHealth · Pontus-X and Gaia-X

    Federated health data space, including compliance work for the Trust List of the Spanish data spaces programme.

  • PROCUREDATAProcurement · Eclipse EDC

    Procurement management with ERP integration and connectors from the Eclipse family.

  • EcoDataNetWaste and circular economy

    Circular-economy traceability with usage policies and verifiable credentials.

Technology we have already deployed: Eclipse Dataspace Components, Dataspace Protocol, Pontus-X, ODRL, verifiable credentials, Gaia-X Trust Framework, FHIR, DICOM, OMOP, Kubernetes, Terraform and Helm.
