Spain's National Security Framework (ENS)

The mandatory cybersecurity framework for the Spanish public sector and its technology suppliers. Without ENS certification it is hard to sell ICT or cloud services to Spanish public bodies. Until the European EUCS scheme and Spain's NIS2 law exist, it is also the most solid national benchmark in Spain.

The ENS is in force and required in Spanish public tenders today. Certification for medium and high categories is issued by an accredited body and renewed with an audit every two years.

Replaces
Royal Decree 3/2010
Legal basis
Law 40/2015 on the Legal Regime of the Public Sector
Scope
The entire Spanish public sector and the suppliers that provide it with services or solutions
Categories
Basic, medium and high
Measures

Technical authority
National Cryptologic Centre (CCN) and CCN-CERT

The essentials in three ideas

1Key to the public market

Tender documents must include ENS requirements when the contracted service supports public systems. Without a certificate, the supplier is left out of many tenders.

Public procurement

2Proportional to risk

The category is set according to the impact on five dimensions: confidentiality, integrity, traceability, authenticity and availability.

Annex I

3A European bridge

The CCN publishes profiles that align the ENS with NIS2 and catalogues of qualified products. The CADA and Spain's data centre royal decree build on it.

CCN-STIC 892
Scope

Who must comply

Public sector

  • Central State Administration, autonomous communities and local authorities
  • Public-law bodies and entities, public universities
  • Systems handling classified information, with their own rules

Private suppliers

  • Companies that develop, maintain or operate systems for the public sector
  • SaaS and cloud supporting public processes or data
  • Managed security services

Not every sale to the public sector triggers the ENS: it depends on the system affected and on what the tender documents require.

Measures are grouped into an organisational framework, an operational framework and protection measures. The specific selection is justified in the statement of applicability. Incidents are reported to CCN-CERT.

References

Market

Hyperscalers and many Spanish providers hold high-category ENS certification. The difference no longer lies in the certificate, but in where the data is and who can access it. Spain's data centre royal decree and the CADA point in that direction.

National cybersecurity

ENS and NIS2

Status

Spain has not transposed NIS2. The draft Cybersecurity Coordination and Governance Law of January 2025 did not reach Parliament before it was dissolved, and Royal Decree-law 12/2018 remains in force. The Commission's infringement procedure is still open.

The bridge

In 2024 the CCN published the specific compliance profile CCN-STIC 892 for entities subject to NIS2. Those who comply with the high-category ENS have much of the NIS2 measures covered.

Across the EU

Public-sector security frameworks in other Member States

The ENS is Spanish. Other countries rely on their own frameworks, usually alongside ISO 27001 and the NIS2 transposition.
