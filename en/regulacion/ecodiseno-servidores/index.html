Ecodesign of servers and data storage

Since 2020 no server or data storage system may be sold in the EU without meeting requirements on power supply efficiency, idle power, secure data deletion, firmware and material information. The rule is being revised and, in parallel, data centres already report their consumption and will get an A–G label.

The 2019 regulation still applies as is: the revision has not yet been adopted. What is new in 2026 mainly affects data centre operators, not the product.

The essentials in three ideas

1Efficient product

Limits on power supply efficiency and idle power, plus active efficiency information measured with SERT.

Annex II, section 1

2Repairable product

Disassembly of key components, built-in secure data deletion, firmware available for eight years and information on critical raw materials.

Annex II, section 3

3Measured data centre

From 500 kW, an annual report on energy, water, waste heat and renewables; from 2027, an A–G rating.

Directive 2023/1791, Art. 12

What it requires of the product

Requirements of the regulation
RequirementContent
Power supplyMinimum efficiency and power factor at different loads
Idle powerA limit that depends on configuration: processors, memory, storage and I/O
Active efficiencyMinimums by server type and publication of results
Temperature classDeclare the operating range (A1 to A4)
Secure deletionBuilt-in function to erase data from all storage devices
DisassemblyKey components removable with commonly available tools
FirmwareLatest version available from two years after placing on the market until eight years after
InformationCobalt content in batteries and neodymium in hard drives

Excluded, among others, are servers with more than four sockets, resilient servers, high-performance computing servers and small-scale servers.

Ecodesign or ESPR

The ESPR (Regulation 2024/1781) replaces the 2009 Directive. Advanced revisions, like this one, can still be published under the old basis if ready by the end of 2026. The ESPR working plan of April 2025 prioritises steel, textiles and other products; servers follow their own track.

Spain

Spain's data centre royal decree

MITECO draft

  • Stems from Royal Decree-law 7/2026
  • Class A for PUE and WUE on the European label, with a transitional maximum PUE
  • 80% of consumption from hourly-matched renewables
  • Sovereignty requirements for public data
  • Centres of 500 kW or more: efficiency data to MITECO

Legislative process

After the August–September 2026 hearing and a critical CNMC report, the text sent to the Council of State raises the threshold from 1 to 10 MW and extends deadlines. The Council of State endorsed it in November with changes, and the Government plans to approve it before the 29 November elections. Details in the royal decree profile.

What it means for each player in the chain

Today

  • Comply with Annex II, CE marking and technical documentation.
  • Firmware available for eight years.

Revision

  • Prepare storage measurement and the possible label.

Today

  • Importer: check CE marking and product information.

Opportunity

  • Catalogue with efficiency data for customers subject to mandatory reporting.

Today

  • Use secure deletion when decommissioning equipment.
  • Help the customer gather the data for the annual report.

Service line

  • Measure PUE and WUE and prepare for the A–G rating.

If you run a data centre

  • From 500 kW: annual report before 15 May.
  • From 2027: A–G rating.

When buying

  • Ask for idle and active efficiency data.
