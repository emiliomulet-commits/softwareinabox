Spanish AI governance law

The organic law bill meant to make the AI Act applicable in Spain: market surveillance authorities, a penalty regime, a sandbox and rules for AI use in the central government sector. It reached the Spanish Congress in June 2026 but lapsed when Parliament was dissolved on 6 October.

There is no Spanish AI law. The AI Act applies directly and the AESIA keeps operating and publishing guidance, but the national penalty regime is missing. The government that emerges from the 29 November elections will have to adopt a new text.

The essentials in three ideas

1The AI Act already binds

The European regulation applies without a national law. Prohibited practices and AI literacy have applied since 2025; general-purpose models since August 2025.

AI Act

2No one yet to fine, and no rules on how

The bill set the authorities and penalties. Without it, authorities can guide and issue warnings, but the national penalty regime remains pending.

Art. 99 AI Act

3It will be back

Any government will have to adopt a national rule: the AI Act requires designating authorities and setting penalties. The lapsed bill is the best guide to what it will contain.

Until there is a law

What applies in Spain today

Does apply

  • The AI Act and its timelines, with the AI Omnibus adjustments
  • The AESIA as a state agency (RD 729/2023), based in A Coruña
  • The AESIA's practical guides for complying with the Regulation
  • GDPR, consumer, labour and product law, as always

Still pending

  • Legal allocation of market surveillance between authorities
  • National regime of infringements and penalties
  • Permanent sandbox run by the AESIA
  • Rules on AI use in the central government sector

A Spanish addition

AI in the central government sector

What it added

  • A title on the "proper use" of AI in public administration
  • AI officers in public bodies
  • Inventory of the AI systems in use
  • Human oversight in significant decisions

For the provider

Although it lapsed, Spanish tender documents already ask for transparency, an inventory and human oversight. Anyone selling AI to Spanish public bodies must be able to explain their system, document it and prove that data stays under control, alongside the ENS.

RD 817/2023

The AI sandbox

First edition

Spain opened the EU's first controlled AI testing environment, with twelve high-risk systems. It produced the practical guides the AESIA publishes today. The edition ended in June 2026.

Next step

The AI Act requires every Member State to have at least one operational sandbox. The bill gave this task to the AESIA and repealed RD 817/2023; with its lapse, the framework for the national sandbox is also pending.

Frequently asked questions

Is Spain's AI governance law in force?

No. The organic law bill reached Congress in June 2026 but lapsed when Parliament was dissolved on 6 October 2026.

Does that mean the AI Act does not apply in Spain?

It does apply: it is a directly applicable EU regulation. What is missing is the national law on authorities, penalties and the use of AI in the public sector.

What should companies do until Spain has an AI law?

Inventory their AI systems, classify them by risk, train staff, use AESIA's guidance and follow the AI Omnibus deadlines for high-risk systems: December 2027 and August 2028.
