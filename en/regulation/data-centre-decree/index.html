Pending in Spain

Spain's data centre royal decree

Spain wants to make large data centres' access to the electricity grid conditional on three sets of requirements: hourly-matched renewable energy, class A energy and water efficiency on the European label, and digital sovereignty. The text has been softened after the public hearing and the CNMC report.

It is a draft, not an approved rule. The Council of State issued its opinion in November 2026 and the Government wants to approve it before the general election on 29 November. As a royal decree, it did not lapse when Parliament was dissolved.

Key facts

Regulation
Draft RD on energy, environmental sustainability, resilience and digital sovereignty requirements for data centres
Enabling provision
First additional provision of Royal Decree-law 7/2026 of 20 March
Ministries
Ecological Transition (MITECO), Economy and Digital Transformation
Legislative process
Urgent; hearing from 27-8 to 10-9-2026
Scope (October text)
Centres with a grid access capacity of 10 MW or more; digital sovereignty regardless of capacity
Consequence
No compliance, no grid access and connection permits, or they are lost
Also implements
Directive (EU) 2023/1791 and Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1364

The essentials in three ideas

1Every megawatt, renewable

80% of consumption covered by new renewables matched hour by hour through self-consumption or forward contracts, until the power system exceeds 90% renewables.

Arts. 7 to 12

2Class A for energy and water

Centres must reach the best class on the European data centre label, due in August 2027, with a transitional maximum PUE.

Efficiency

3Public data in the EU

Centres hosting sensitive public systems will have to process, store and back up that data only in the European Union.

Art. 5

Content

The four blocks of requirements

Requirements of the draft
BlockWhat it requires
Resilience and digital sovereigntySelf-declaration before starting operations; requirements on establishment, data location, traceability and subcontractors
Energy and water efficiencyClass A for PUE and WUE on the European label; reuse of waste heat
Renewable energy80% of consumption from self-consumption or forward renewable purchase contracts, with additionality and hourly correlation
InformationCentres of 500 kW or more send MITECO data on efficiency, renewables, water footprint and demand, which is published

Serious, uncorrected non-compliance can lead to loss of grid access and connection permits. Pending applications must prove compliance within a short period or are refused.

Article 5

Digital sovereignty

August text

  • Operator established in the EU
  • Operational data and metadata in the EU
  • Public sector systems subject to the ENS: data, metadata, logs, replicas and backups only in the EU

October text

  • Focuses on centres hosting HIGH-category ENS public systems
  • Metadata and telemetry are excluded
  • Remote technical support from third countries is allowed where indispensable

The European Commission has asked the Government to check whether it must notify the text as a technical regulation. Some 12 GW of grid access permits have been granted to data centres, according to the CNMC.

Frequently asked questions

Has Spain's data centre decree been approved?

Not yet. It is a draft processed under the urgent procedure, with a public hearing from 27-8 to 10-9-2026; approval by the Council of Ministers is expected in November 2026.

Which data centres would the decree apply to?

Under the October text, those with an access capacity of 10 MW or more; the digital sovereignty requirements apply regardless of capacity.

What happens if a data centre does not meet the requirements?

Grid access and connection permits are not granted, or those already granted are lost.
