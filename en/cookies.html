Cookie policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as the browser's local storage, serve similar purposes.

2. What we use on these sites

The sites www.softwareinabox.eu, cognitiv.softwareinabox.eu, argos.softwareinabox.eu and datos.softwareinabox.eu do not set first-party or third-party cookies for analytics, advertising or tracking, and do not load external measurement services.

TechnologyPurposeTypeDuration
Browser local storage (only on some pages)Remembering interface preferences, such as the language or the state of a demo conversationTechnical, first-party, exempt from consentUntil you delete it from your browser

For this reason we do not display a cookie consent banner. If in future we add analytics tools or other non-technical cookies, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

3. How to manage them

You can delete or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings. See your browser's help pages for instructions.

4. More information

If you have any questions, write to emilio.mulet@softwareinabox.eu. See also our privacy policy and legal notice.
