3. How long we keep it

Contact data is kept while your enquiry is being handled and, afterwards, for the period needed to deal with any legal liabilities. If a business relationship begins, it will be kept for the duration of that relationship and the subsequent statutory periods.

4. Who can access the data

We do not disclose data to third parties unless required by law. To provide the service we work with the following providers, who act as data processors or technical service providers:

  • GitHub, Inc. (hosting of the sites via GitHub Pages).
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (domain name resolution).
  • FormSubmit (forwarding form messages to our mailbox).
  • Our email provider, to receive and answer messages.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. In that case, the transfer is covered by a European Commission adequacy decision (such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or by standard contractual clauses.

Web fonts are served from our own domain, so your browser does not connect to third-party font services.

5. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability, and withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to emilio.mulet@softwareinabox.eu and stating the right you wish to exercise. If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

6. Security

All sites are served exclusively over HTTPS. We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk to protect data against unauthorised access, loss or alteration.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change the way we process data (for example, by adding web analytics), we will update this page and state the date of the latest revision.
